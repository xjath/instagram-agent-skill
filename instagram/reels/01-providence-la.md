## Facts
- Restaurant: Providence, Los Angeles. Seafood-focused tasting menu, chef Michael Cimarusti.
- Visited: January 2026, dinner for two.
- Menus start at US$375 per person; they had the chef's tasting menu at
  US$495 per person (clarified by the user 2026-11-22).
- Bill: about CA$3,400 total = roughly US$2,400 for two (CAD to USD at 0.699, 2026-11-18). Includes two wine pairings, a US$100 cocktail, menu add-ons, tax and tip.
- Verdict: WORTH IT, definitely.
- Moments: service attentive all night without hovering or being overbearing; every dish a "flavor bomb"; they gave us a private room with a view right into the kitchen; took us on a wine cellar tour.
- Letdowns: none.
- Footage: wine cellar, the room, the food, the menu.
- Also seen (user, 2026-11-22/23): invited to the wine cellar at check-in; a custom Dom Pérignon bottle with
  three Michelin stars on it, to celebrate the third star. Photo confirmed

## Detail answers (user, 2026-11-23)
1. Private room: chefs working in the kitchen, dinner being made right in
   front of them for the people dining in the restaurant.
2. Cellar: smaller than expected; the sommelier gave a tour; rows and rows of
   bottles on wooden wine shelves.
3. Service: the bartender who made their drink knew how to tell a story; the
   staff who served them were very informative, funny, and made them feel
   welcome and at home.
4. Food: the black bass was a favourite: simple, delicate, yet unexpectedly
   flavourful.
5. End of the night: full and happy. Many restaurants start strong and fall
   off; Providence kept the level the hors d'oeuvres set, all the way through.
6. Would go back with anyone; "it's just that good".
