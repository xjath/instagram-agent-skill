Saved 2026-11-20 from the user's notes. Every skill reads this before writing.

## The account
- Instagram account @theluxverdict: a new luxury lifestyle account, separate
  from the user's personal account. Never cross-post to or reference the
  personal account.
- Angle: "is it actually worth it?" with real prices and honest verdicts. Global.
- Format: voiceover over B-roll. No face on camera.
- Current series: an 18-reel Michelin series (11 starred restaurants, 9 Michelin
  Key hotels visited in 2026). Providence is post #1. Places are in `backlog.md`.

## Voice rules
- Loose, run-on, conversational. Chain thoughts with "so", "and", "because".
  Casual filler OK: "okay so", "honestly", "wanna", "gonna".
- Never clipped robotic one-liners in a row. React to food instead of labelling it.
- Never critic words: unctuous, mouthfeel, "a symphony of", elevated, curated.
- No emoji. Burned-in word-by-word captions, key word in red.
- Never invent facts; verify accolades before using them.
